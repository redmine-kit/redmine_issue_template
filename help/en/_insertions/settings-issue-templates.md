## Issue templates

The **Issue templates** tab, shown to members with **Manage issue templates** when the module **Issue templates** is on, lists the project's [issue templates](../issue-template/issue-templates.md#The-Issue-templates-tab) and where they come from, with **Import** for a repository, **New template** when they are edited in Redmine, the chooser's settings (**Allow blank issues**, **Other contacts**) and **Sharing**.
