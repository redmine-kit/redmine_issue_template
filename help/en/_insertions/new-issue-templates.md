### Issue templates

When the project has [issue templates](../issue-template/issue-templates.md), **New issue** first opens the template chooser: the templates with their **Name**, **Description** and **Tracker**, **Blank issue** when the project allows blank issues, and **Other contacts**. The chosen template fills in the form: its tracker, subject and description, and the fields it sets, which can't be changed. A YAML form shows its own fields at the top of the form and writes the answers into the description.
